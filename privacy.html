Privacy policy

Ivy Assistant is used only with one dedicated test Google account that contains synthetic data.

  • Data accessed: Gmail messages and drafts, and Google Calendar events, of that test account only.
  • Use: to summarize email, list events, find free time and create draft replies for the account owner. Emails are sent only after explicit approval.
  • Storage: an OAuth token is stored in Google Secret Manager and a private Google Cloud Storage bucket. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the Google account's security settings.
